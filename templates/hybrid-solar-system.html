A MORE FLEXIBLE ENERGY SETUP

Solar generation with energy storage

Solar panels can produce more electricity during the day than your household needs at that moment. A battery can capture some of that surplus for use at another time.

Instead of relying only on the electricity generated at the time your panels are producing, stored energy can become another source for your household when solar production falls.

Your property can remain connected to the electricity network, giving you another energy source when your solar generation and stored energy are not sufficient.

WHY ADD BATTERY STORAGE?

Get more flexibility from your solar system

Adding storage can change the way you use the electricity your solar system produces. Instead of using solar only while it is being generated, you can keep some energy for later.

This can be particularly useful for households where a significant portion of electricity consumption occurs after daylight hours.

The right battery size depends on your energy consumption, solar generation, household requirements and how you intend to use the system.
